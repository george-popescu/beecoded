# Polish

Use the actual audience, locale, product facts and brand voice. Work on the requested
text or interface; a review produces findings, a rewrite produces usable copy, and
implementation changes only the authorized surface. Preserve technical meaning,
claims, prices, eligibility, consent, links, localization keys and placeholders.

Write as someone who understands the product and the reader's task. Prefer concrete
information and direct actions. Remove unsupported praise, filler, repetitive
rhythm and decoration that obscures meaning. Match formality to the context;
natural language need not be chatty. Do not fabricate testimonials, experience,
statistics or provenance to make content feel human. Preserve required disclosures.

Read the result for plausible unintended meanings in its language and context:
sexual or insulting associations, slang, ambiguous referents, missing qualifiers
and misleading promises. Check short headings, CTAs and isolated labels as well as
whole sentences. Rewrite the phrase or sentence until the intended meaning is
clear. Use local idiom and correct diacritics, especially in Romanian. Do not rely
on a word blacklist, invent remote interpretations or remove legitimate terminology
whose meaning is clear. Ask only when the intended product meaning is unresolved.

For design work, use existing brand/design context and the user's task to choose
hierarchy, layout, imagery and interaction. Preserve useful conventions and
accessibility. Do not add novelty, animation or a new visual system just to look
less generic. Inspect the rendered result when assessing appearance; source-only
or described designs support recommendations, not claims of visual verification.

Keep small edits exact: a color, title or spacing request does not authorize nearby
rewrites, research, a new concept or new documents. For a substantial new surface
or redesign, diagnose the current experience, reuse approved direction and select
relevant visual references only where they answer a design question. Explain the
transferable principle briefly, choose a product-specific concept and make the
real offer or task visible in the result. Compare the rendered outcome with that
intent. No reference quota, image generation, 3D or extra approval is required.
Do not invent services or promises to fill a layout.

Finish with the revised text or implemented result and only material explanations.
Recheck meaning, ambiguity and fit once in context; rendered checks should cover
affected labels, states and viewports. Do not create permanent exact-copy tests.
Style patterns do not establish AI authorship, and this skill does not evade
detectors or certify content as human-written. The optional
[research notes](../../references/polish-research.md) explain the source limits.
