# Polish research notes

Consulted 2026-10-07. These sources inform editorial judgment; they are not a
detector, a checklist of prohibited words, or mandatory reading for each rewrite.
The skill's instructions are an original synthesis tailored to product work.

- [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)
  is a WikiProject advice page, not an encyclopedia article or official policy.
  It describes patterns such as inflated significance, unsupported attribution
  and repetitive presentation. Those observations do not prove authorship and
  some apply specifically to Wikipedia. Product copy can legitimately persuade;
  the requirement here is truthful, clear and context-appropriate language.
- [Signs of AI design](https://github.com/febbhav/signs-of-ai-design) is an independent
  community repository, not a Wikipedia publication or a validated detection model.
  Use its discussion of repeated visual defaults to question whether a design
  serves this product. A familiar font, card layout or gradient is not a defect
  by itself. Preserve deliberate brand choices and working interaction conventions.
- [NN/G: Plain Language Is for Everyone, Even Experts](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/plain-language-experts/)
  supports clear audience-appropriate wording without unnecessary complexity.
- [NN/G: UX-Context Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-context-design/)
  argues for grounding generated interfaces in real users, domain constraints and
  design knowledge. Treat its developing practice as guidance, not a universal
  performance guarantee. Reuse relevant existing context; do not require a new
  context document for a small task.

Ambiguity review is an additional product requirement. Judge phrases in their
locale, audience and display context; a word can be appropriate in one sentence
and distracting or misleading in another. Reword the intended meaning rather than
applying universal substitutions. Deliberate humor remains a product choice, but
do not introduce accidental innuendo into otherwise straightforward copy.

Assess language by meaning and usability. For appearance, use a browser or supplied
visuals and state what was actually observed. Do not replace user research with
invented personas, fabricated feedback or a score claiming the work is human.
